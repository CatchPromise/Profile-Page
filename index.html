Wizkid

ARTIST

Wizkid is a Grammy Award-winning Nigerian singer and songwriter widely celebrated as one of the most prominent pioneers of the global Afrobeats movement. He began recording music at just 11 years old and achieved a massive national breakthrough in 2011 under Banky W's E.M.E record label with his chart-topping debut album, Superstar.

Key Career Milestones

  • 2011: Released his debut studio album, Superstar, launching him into African superstardom.
  • 2016: Attained massive global recognition co-writing and featuring on Drake's worldwide number-one hit "One Dance".
  • 2021: Won a Grammy Award for Best Music Video for his performance on Beyoncé's "Brown Skin Girl".
  • 2024: Released Morayo, his deeply personal sixth studio album dedicated to his late mother.
  • 2026: Inducted into the prestigious Africa Hall of Fame as the first living Afrobeats artist to receive the honor.
